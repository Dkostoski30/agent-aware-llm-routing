How to read it
A hexagon in the agent runtime is one agent task. Its fill grows with its context, the dots around it are its steps, a turning gear a tool call.
A capsule is one LLM call, longer when it carries more context; a light going back is the reply.
The glass tank on a pod is its KV cache, filled from the bottom: one cell per block, bright while a call uses it, dim when kept for later, grey for a finished run.
Under each call in a pod's bays: amber while the pod rereads the prompt, teal while it writes the reply.
