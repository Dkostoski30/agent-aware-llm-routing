# P5.3: E3, a pod killed mid-run, with the retry off and on, over four seeds

Planned 2026-11-26, on branch `feature/phase-5`, after P5.2 (card 76).

Goal:
PLAN.md's P5.3 row: "pod killed mid-run, retry off and on, more than one seed"; measured: "completion rate and
recovery cost (extra seconds per affected run, calls sent again)". P4.4 (card 60) built the kill and the retry and ran
them once, on simulated pods at seed 6 on P3.9b's `burst`: 27 of 30 runs completed with the retry off, 30 of 30 with it
on. It had no recovery cost. This card runs the kill at four seeds on P5's load and adds that cost.

Depends on:
P4.4 (`make failover-check`, the retry rule, the report), P4.4b (the error chunk read as a 5xx), card 73 (the seeds),
cards 74-76 (the load).

Read in the code before planning (Claude, 2026-11-26):
- `loadgen/load_generator/failover.py` already kills the Ready pod holding the most live pins with a force delete at
  grace 0, kill_after_seconds into the replay, and counts the runs that complete, the runs failed by the kill, the runs
  failed on their deadline only, each call running on the killed pod at the kill with its outcome, the retries and the
  runs remapped off the pod. It takes one seed per file and has no reference to compare run times with.
- It has none of `make experiment`'s real-pod checks (the GPU blockers guard, the GPU logs, the stall check, starting
  the pods one at a time, the warm-up), and it restarts both pods together, which froze two compiled pods in card 74.
- A replay line's `gap_before_ms` runs from the step before's end to the call's last attempt (`replay._call`), so a
  retried later call's failed attempt and backoff are inside its gap; a first call's gap is 0.
- At seeds 6, 9, 10 and 13 `burst-fan-out-heavy`'s bursts come at 120, 240, 360, 480 s (and 600 s at seed 10).

Decided (the user, 2026-11-26):
1. **Simulated pods only** (offered first: real 2 pods). The kill's question is the retry's, which P4.4 checked on both
   real and simulated pods; on real it would need the real-pod checks above added first. The simulated times are
   optimistic (Gate 2, P2.9), which the thesis says.
2. **Recovery cost against a no-kill replay** (Claude's recommendation): a third variant per seed, the same replay with
   the retry on and nothing killed. Each run's time in a kill variant less its time in the no-kill one is its extra
   time. Offered instead: the retry lines' lost time alone, which misses the cache misses after a remap and the pod lost.
3. **`make failover-check` extended** (recommended), not a kill inside `make experiment`, whose core loop every P5 square
   ran on.
4. **P5's load, the kill 5 s into the second burst** (recommended): `burst-fan-out-heavy`, `kill_after_seconds: 245`,
   both pods warm and loaded. Offered: P4.4's `burst` with the kill at 125 s. A run that fails on its deadline while one
   pod carries the load is counted apart, as in P4.4.

The plan as approved ("go", 2026-11-26):
- One file, `experiments/p5-3-failover.yaml`, with `seeds:` 6, 9, 10, 13, each with its own order of the three
  variants:

  | seed | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 6 | retry-off | retry-on | no-kill |
  | 9 | no-kill | retry-off | retry-on |
  | 10 | retry-on | no-kill | retry-off |
  | 13 | no-kill | retry-on | retry-off |

  Seeds 6, 9 and 10 are a Latin square; retry-on runs before no-kill at two seeds and after it at two.
- **The verdict, fixed before the runs:** retry-on passes a seed with at least one call running on the killed pod at
  the kill and no run failed by the kill (P4.4's rule). P5.3 is green when retry-on passes at all four seeds. retry-off
  is the control, counted, not judged. A seed with no call on the killed pod is void, named as a limit. The recovery
  cost is reported, not judged.

Claude's choices:
- **A file of seeds** is one experiment per seed, its results named `<name>-<hash>-s<seed>-*` and its `.meta.json`
  written after its last variant. A seed with a `.meta.json` is skipped, so the same command picks up a run that
  stopped; a seed with other files but no `.meta.json` is refused before anything runs; a file whose every seed is
  finished is refused, as P4.4's was. A file of one `seed:` is P4.4's form and keeps its results' names.
- **The no-kill variant** (`kill: false`) starts no kill timer and waits for no replacement. It must retry, as it is
  the retry's reference; a file has at most one, and at least one variant that kills.
- **A run's time** is each step's slowest call plus its gap, summed, on the replay's monotonic clock, as
  `results.run_completion_ms` counts it, plus a retried first call's lost time, which no gap holds. A step's lost time
  runs from its first attempt's sent time to its last attempt's start, on the wall clock both are printed on.
- **The recovery cost** pairs each run that finished in both the kill variant and the no-kill one, in three groups,
  each run in the first it fits: a call running on the killed pod at the kill; pinned to the killed pod at the kill and
  not finished; the rest, whose extra time is the noise between two replays plus one pod carrying the load. Per group:
  runs, median and max extra seconds. Also the runs that did not finish on one side, the steps sent again and their
  lost time in all.
- Each `.meta.json` gets the source state `make experiment` writes (commit, uncommitted paths, system hash), each
  variant its `kill` and a `verdict` (control, passed, failed, void, reference), and a kill variant its `recovery`.
- After the run, `<name>-<hash>-summary.csv` is written from every seed's `.meta.json`, a row per seed and variant, and
  each variant's completion rate over the seeds (mean, lowest, highest) is printed. The CSV is derived and written
  again on each run.
- No warm-up, as P4.4: completion counts every run, and the cost pairs runs one by one, so a cold first run is in both
  sides.

Files:
- `loadgen/load_generator/failover.py`: seeds, the no-kill variant, run times, lost time, the recovery cost, the
  summary; `loadgen/tests/test_failover.py`: 24 new tests, P4.4's 44 unchanged (no line of the file removed).
- `Makefile`: `failover-check`'s comment.
- `experiments/p5-3-failover.yaml` (new); `experiments/tests/test_p5_3.py` (new, 5 tests: the squares' load, trace and
  seeds on sim, the kill 5 s into the second burst at every seed, P4.4's wait and settle, the Latin rows, retry-on
  before no-kill at two seeds).
- This card, PLAN.md's P5.3 row and the `experiments/log.md` entry, after the run.

Done when:
- `make test` and `make lint` pass.
- `make failover-check EXPERIMENT=experiments/p5-3-failover.yaml` runs the 12 replays and writes four `.meta.json`
  files and the summary CSV. Whatever the verdicts say goes in the log, with each seed's completion per variant, the
  calls on the killed pod, the retries and the recovery cost by group, the simulated times labelled optimistic.

Run (the user, laptop idle, Windows' AC standby off; about 2.5-3 h: 12 replays of ~10-12 min, each kill variant then
waits ~140 s for the replacement):

```bash
cd ~/thesis
make test
make lint
make down
make up PROFILE=sim
make failover-check EXPERIMENT=experiments/p5-3-failover.yaml
```

If it stops partway, the same last command skips the finished seeds; a seed left halfway has to have its files moved
out first, as the error names them.

Result (2026-11-27, red by the user's call after the run; file hash 4be74dd7b3f5, system hash 4191516da9e7 at all four
seeds; full numbers in `experiments/log.md`): 12 replays, no kill or replacement error, 0 deadline failures, the picker
never restarted.

| seed | retry-off: calls on the killed pod, runs failed, completion | retry-on: calls on the killed pod, retried, completion |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | 7, 0, 50 of 50 | 6, 0, 50 of 50 |
| 9 | 5, 0, 50 of 50 | 4, 0, 50 of 50 |
| 10 | 11, 2, 48 of 50 | 7, 0, 50 of 50 |
| 13 | 7, 1, 49 of 50 | 9, 0, 50 of 50 |

By the verdict fixed before the runs retry-on passed at all four seeds, but it sent 0 calls again: every call on the
killed pod was answered by that pod 10 ms to 3.0 s after the delete, the window P4.4 saw, and calls here take a median
of 1.0-1.9 s. retry-off lost the calls that ran past it (3 cut mid-reply, 1 gateway 503). The rule counted calls running
on the killed pod, not calls the kill cut. The recovery cost against no-kill: runs with a call on the killed pod
0.5-7.7 s longer at the median (max 19.2 s), the rest's median 0.0-1.5 s and max 16.5-33.7 s, so the noise between two
replays is as large as the cost. Simulated times, optimistic. The user chose red and a follow-up card, P5.3b, over green
with the limit stated or inconclusive on the retry.
