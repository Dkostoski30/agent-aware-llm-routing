# P3.9b: KEDA scales on remaining work

The risk to know before running: a change in remaining work reaches a new replica after one 5 s Prometheus scrape, KEDA's polling, and Kubernetes' HPA loop (15 s by default). On a fast burst the queue can pass its threshold inside that delay even though remaining work crossed its target first. The CSV therefore records both moments: when the signal crossed, and when the scale-up began.
