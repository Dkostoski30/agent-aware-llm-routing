# P5.3b: E3 again, with a kill that cuts every call on the pod at once

Planned 2026-11-27, on branch `feature/phase-5`, after P5.3 (card 77).

Goal:
P5.3 ran the kill at four seeds and was closed red by the user (2026-11-27): retry-on completed 50 of 50 at every seed,
but it sent 0 calls again. Every call on the force-deleted pod was answered by it 10 ms to 3.0 s after the delete, in
the seconds the kubelet leaves a pod (P4.4's window), and calls on this load take a median of 1.0-1.9 s. The verdict rule
counted calls running on the killed pod, not calls the kill cut. This card makes the kill cut every call on the pod and
judges the retry on the cut calls.

Depends on:
P5.3 (card 77: the seeds form, the no-kill reference, the recovery cost, the file), P4.4 (the kill, the retry, the
report).

Decided (the user, 2026-11-27):
1. **Stop the pod's containers at once, then force-delete it** (recommended): `crictl stop --timeout 0` on the pod's
   running containers, which containerd turns into SIGKILL with no SIGTERM before it, then P5.3's delete, so the
   ReplicaSet replaces the pod. A crash or an OOM kill, not a shutdown. Offered: P5.3's kill with seeds added until four
   cut a call (in P5.3 two of four retry-off replays did, ~8 seeds, no guarantee); P5.3's kill timed for the pod's
   longest calls (only moves the odds).
2. **All three variants again** (recommended): retry-off, retry-on, no-kill, so the recovery cost includes calls sent
   again. Offered: retry-on and no-kill only; retry-off and retry-on only.
3. **The verdict counts cut calls** (recommended), fixed before the run: retry-on passes a seed when the kill cut at
   least one call, the retry sent it again and no run failed by the kill. A seed with no cut call is void, named as a
   limit. P5.3b is green when retry-on passes at all four seeds. retry-off is the control; the recovery cost is reported,
   not judged. Offered: P5.3's rule (with an immediate kill the two agree on every call running at the kill).

The plan as approved ("go", 2026-11-27):
- `experiments/p5-3b-failover.yaml` is P5.3's file with `stop_container: true` and `require_cut: true`, the same seeds
  (6, 9, 10, 13) and orders, so each seed's no-kill replay can be set beside P5.3's: two replays of the same seed and
  variant, the noise between them.
- Both keys default to false, so P4.4's and P5.3's files keep their kill and verdict.

Claude's choices:
- **A cut call** is a call on the killed pod, running at the kill or sent during the settle (one sent after the kill
  before the picker learnt of it, as `hotpotqa-85-29` step 3 at P5.3's seed 13), with an attempt that failed for a
  reason but its deadline. A call's pod is the one the report already places it on (P4.4: its pick, else its run's pin).
- **The stop** finds the pod's one ready sandbox (`crictl pods --name ^<pod>$ --state ready`), stops its running
  containers, then the delete runs. A failed crictl, or a pod with no single ready sandbox, is kept as the kill's error
  and nothing is deleted, as a failed delete is.
- **sudo:** containerd's socket is root's, so crictl runs as `sudo -n k3s crictl`. When the file stops containers,
  `crictl version` runs before anything else, and a failure refuses the run with "run sudo -v first". The run block keeps
  sudo alive, as for `make up`.
- Each report prints the cut calls under the calls running at the kill; each kill variant's `.meta.json` entry has
  `cut`, the `.meta.json` has `stop_container` and `require_cut`, and the summary CSV a `cut_calls` column (blank for
  P5.3's files, whose metas have no cut list).
